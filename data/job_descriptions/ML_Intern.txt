Machine Learning Intern

About the role
We are looking for a Machine Learning Intern to assist our team in developing and evaluating machine learning models for real-world business problems.

Responsibilities

* Collect, clean, and preprocess structured datasets
* Perform exploratory data analysis and feature engineering
* Train and evaluate supervised and unsupervised machine learning models
* Experiment with algorithms such as Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and clustering
* Tune model hyperparameters and compare model performance
* Prepare technical documentation and communicate findings
* Assist in deploying ML models through APIs

Requirements

* Pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Data Science, or a related field
* Good knowledge of Python
* Familiarity with Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn
* Understanding of regression, classification, and clustering
* Basic knowledge of SQL
* Understanding of model evaluation metrics
* Familiarity with Git/GitHub

Good to have

* FastAPI or Flask
* XGBoost
* Docker
* Basic knowledge of cloud deployment
* Experience with an end-to-end ML project

Duration: 3–6 months
Work mode: Remote/Hybrid
