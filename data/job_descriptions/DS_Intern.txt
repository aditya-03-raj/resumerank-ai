Data Science Intern

About the role
We are seeking a Data Science Intern who enjoys working with data and extracting meaningful insights to support data-driven decisions.

Responsibilities

* Clean and preprocess large datasets
* Perform exploratory data analysis
* Identify trends, patterns, and relationships in data
* Build statistical and machine learning models
* Create meaningful visualizations and reports
* Perform feature engineering and model evaluation
* Work with structured datasets using Python and SQL
* Present analytical findings to the team

Requirements

* Pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field
* Strong Python fundamentals
* Experience with Pandas and NumPy
* Knowledge of Scikit-learn
* Understanding of statistics and probability
* Knowledge of SQL
* Experience with data visualization using Matplotlib, Seaborn, or similar tools
* Understanding of regression and classification

Good to have

* Power BI or Tableau
* Experience with XGBoost or ensemble models
* Knowledge of time-series analysis
* Experience working on real-world datasets
* Familiarity with Git

Duration: 3–6 months
Work mode: Remote
